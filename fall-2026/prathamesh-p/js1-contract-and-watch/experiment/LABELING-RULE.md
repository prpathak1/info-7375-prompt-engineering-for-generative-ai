# Labeling rule for "real fit for this persona"

> **Provenance.** Claude Code wrote the first draft on 2026-10-30, saved as `LABELING-RULE-DRAFT.md`. On 2026-10-31, Claude (claude.ai chat) drafted the
> seniority decisions below, and Prathamesh reviewed and approved them. **Approved by Prathamesh, 2026-10-31.** This rule is **separate from the CONTRACT rule**
> (CONTRACT §4). The CONTRACT rule is the deterministic record. This rule defines the human labels for the prompt experiment.

A posting is labeled **fit** for the fictional persona only if all three of the following hold. The persona is an F-1 master's student in network engineering who
needs sponsorship, has about 3 years of pre-degree network-engineering work, and holds no clearance.

**(1) Network-role fit:** the core of the job is designing, building, operating or securing computer networks (SOC 15-1241 work), judged from the duties rather
than only the title. Software, firmware or systems roles that merely touch networking are not fit.

**(2) Work authorization:** nothing in the posting rules out an F-1 holder who needs sponsorship. There is no U.S.-citizenship, U.S.-person/ITAR/EAR, or
security-clearance requirement, read in context rather than by keyword.

**(3) Seniority:** the role is realistic for a new master's graduate entering on OPT:
- **Up to 3 years required (including "3+ years") is acceptable.** **4 or more required years is not fit.**
- A **Senior, Staff, Principal, Lead, Manager or Director** title is **not fit, even with no years stated**.
- **Only required years count.** Preferred or "nice to have" years do not count toward the cutoff.

Everything else (location, salary, team) is ignored. When a posting is genuinely ambiguous on one of the three points, label it not fit and note which point was unclear.
